# **Goal**: learn to recognize and address problems that arise during model training.

**Task**: build a multilayer classifier and divide the data into training, validation, and test sets. Prepare a simple baseline model. Then deliberately produce at least two problems, such as overfitting, underfitting, or unstable training, and propose ways to reduce them.

**Experiment**: compare at least three configurations while varying one chosen factor. Possible factors include the learning rate, optimizer, dropout probability, weight decay, and batch size.

**Measures and analysis**: report measures appropriate for the classification task and plot training and validation loss. Explain the causes of the problems and show whether your changes reduced them. Use the evidence to justify your final configuration.
